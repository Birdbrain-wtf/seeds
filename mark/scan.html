Read a seed mark

Hold a seed mark inside the circle. The picture stays on this device: the reading happens here and nothing is sent anywhere.

The camera starts when you ask it to.

A seed mark carries 32 bytes and a 16-bit check, drawn as four rings and an outer ring. The reader accepts a picture only when the check matches, so a misread never comes back as someone else. How it works: the mark in the Seeds repository.
